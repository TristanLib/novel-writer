- Start-state ledger

- Promise/payoff map

## Promise/payoff map

| Promise ID | First plant | Reader expectation | Intermediate dividends | Final payoff window | Payoff-created pressure | End state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use stable IDs such as `V02-P01`. End state should be `closed`,
`transformed`, or `carry-forward`; add the next valid window to every
carry-forward item.
